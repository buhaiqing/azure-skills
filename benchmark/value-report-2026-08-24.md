## Calculation Methodology

### Cost Savings
```
cost_saved = heal_count × $50/incident
```
- Assumption: Average manual Azure operations cost ~$50/incident (labor + context-switching)
- Source: Industry average for cloud operations labor (AWS/Azure support teams)

### Time Savings
```
time_saved = heal_count × (avg_manual_mttr - avg_auto_mttr) / 60
         = heal_count × (15min - 0.5min) / 60
```
- avg_manual_mttr: 15 minutes (typical human diagnose → fix → verify cycle)
- avg_auto_mttr: 0.5 minutes (auto-feedback loop median from trace data)

### Recovery Time Delta
```
delta = avg_manual_mttr - avg_auto_mttr = 15min - 0.5min = 14.5min
```
- This is the per-incident time advantage of autonomous healing

### Availability Impact
```
availability = heal_count × time_saved_per_incident / 60
```
- Translates recovery time savings into availability hours
